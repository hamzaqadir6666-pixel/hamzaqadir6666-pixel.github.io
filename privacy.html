Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This policy explains exactly what DeviceLens ("the app") does and does not do with information on your device. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

1. Device information stays on your device

DeviceLens reads hardware and system details — processor, GPU, memory, storage, battery, display, sensors, cameras, network state and operating system version — using standard Android APIs. All of it is processed locally and displayed only on your screen. The developer operates no servers and receives none of this information.

2. Information we never access

  • IMEI, MEID or device serial number
  • Subscriber ID (IMSI) or phone number
  • Android ID or any persistent hardware identifier
  • Contacts, messages, photos, files or call logs
  • Your geographic location — even when location permission is granted

3. Advertising

The app is free and supported by advertising provided by Start.io (formerly StartApp). To serve ads, the Start.io SDK connects to the internet and may process:

  • Your device's resettable advertising ID, if you consented to personalized ads
  • Coarse technical data such as country, language, device model and OS version
  • Ad interaction events (an ad was shown, or tapped)

On first launch the app asks whether you want personalized ads. If you decline, the advertising ID is not used for targeting. You can change this at any time in Settings › Advertising, and you can control or reset the advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads.

Start.io's own privacy policy is available at start.io/policy/privacy-policy-site. Your hardware report is never sent to Start.io or any advertiser.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Internet / Network stateServing ads, and reporting your connection type
Wi‑Fi stateReading link speed, frequency and standard
Advertising IDAd delivery, only with your consent
Location (optional)Android requires it before any app can read the connected Wi‑Fi network name. Your location is never read or stored.
Nearby devices (optional)Reading the Bluetooth adapter name and state on Android 12+. No scanning or pairing.

Declining either optional permission simply shows "Unavailable" for those fields. Everything else keeps working.

5. Data you create

Device snapshots you save are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Reports you export as TXT or JSON are written to a temporary cache folder and are shared only when you choose a destination in the Android share sheet. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

6. Children

DeviceLens is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. No information is knowingly collected from children.

7. Your rights

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete. For advertising data, you may withdraw consent in the app at any time, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
