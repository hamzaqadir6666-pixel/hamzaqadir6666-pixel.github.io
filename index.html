DeviceLens

Know your hardware.

DeviceLens is a fast, private Android hardware inspector. It reads your device's real specifications directly from the Android platform and presents them in one clean place — no guesswork, no hardcoded values.

Processed on-device No tracking SDKs Works offline

What it shows

SoC & CPUCores, clusters, live clocks and load
GPURenderer, OpenGL ES, Vulkan, extensions
BatteryHealth, temperature, voltage, current
DisplayResolution, refresh modes, HDR
MemoryRAM, storage, swap and ZRAM
SensorsFull list plus live testing
NetworkWi‑Fi, mobile and Bluetooth
CameraLenses, sensor size, apertures

Privacy

Your hardware information is read and displayed locally. It is never uploaded, sold, or shared with advertisers. DeviceLens contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and never reads your IMEI, serial number, subscriber ID, phone number or Android ID.

The app is free and supported by ads. Read the full privacy policy.
